Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Reels Crosspost ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Ivan Pankin. It publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What data the app accesses

  • With the youtube.upload scope, the app uploads videos, titles, descriptions and tags that the owner prepared to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • With the youtube.readonly scope, the app reads the processing and privacy status of the videos it uploaded and the name of the connected channel.

The app does not access other channels, comments, subscribers, analytics or any data of other people.

How data is stored and used

  • The OAuth refresh token is stored only as a secret variable on the owner's private server and is used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • For each published video the app stores its YouTube video ID, link and status in the owner's private database, to show a publishing report to the owner.
  • Video files are kept in the owner's storage and are deleted from the server's execution history after 7 days.

Sharing

Data obtained through YouTube API Services is not sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party.

Cookies

The app does not place cookies or tracking technologies on users' devices.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. After revocation the stored token stops working, and the stored video IDs and links are deleted on request within 7 days.
